# Aluco – Name

Decided: 2026-11-18

**Aluco** – from the Latin species name of the tawny owl, *Strix aluco*. The owl keeps watch at night and notices every movement; in German, a "komischer Kauz" (an odd owl) is someone who stands out from the crowd – like the events the tool finds.

- Tagline: **"Aluco – a home radar for your network"**
- Logo idea: owl / tawny owl
- CLI: `aluco block tv`
- Page name in the dashboard for the device overview: "Radar"
